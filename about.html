About EC Marketing Corporation

Learn about our story, vision, mission, and the products we carry.

Our story

EC Marketing Corporation, registered as Calbayog E.C. Marketing, Incorporated, started selling grocery and motorcycle items at the old market area. The business later moved to Gomez Street and became a corporation in 2003. Today it serves Calbayog City as a wholesale and retail supplier of construction and hardware materials.

Vision

To be a leading and trusted business corporation with diversified holdings in various investments and interests while upholding established family values and traditions.

Mission

To become a leading brand and trusted business corporation engaging in diversified ventures through family support and customer patronage toward a prosperous and stable future for current and succeeding generations.

What we carry

Wholesale and retail construction and hardware materials, including cement and steel bars, water storage systems (authorized supplier of Bestank and Firstank stainless steel tanks, 400L to 4000L), Bosch power tools (drills, angle grinders, demolition hammers, planers, saws), and general hardware (plumbing fixtures, electrical wiring supplies, fasteners, paints, outdoor garden supplies).

Brands and quality

We offer quality products from DTI-certified brands, including Bestank, Firstank, and Bosch, at reasonable prices.

Who we serve

  • Contractors
  • Construction companies
  • Stores that sell construction and hardware products
  • Walk-in retail customers and homeowners

Eco practice

No single-use plastic in the store, with eco-bags and paper bags.
