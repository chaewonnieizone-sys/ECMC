How Ordering Works

Follow the steps from product discovery to feedback. Our team confirms availability, price, and delivery before finalizing orders.

1. Discover products

Browse categories, search the catalog, and view product details for construction materials, water tanks, power tools, and hardware & paint.

2. Inquire

Ask about availability or details using the product inquiry, contact form, or by calling, texting, or emailing the shop.

3. Price and availability confirmation

The shop confirms item availability, final pricing, and delivery details before you proceed.

4. Order

Prepare your order or bulk quote message on the site and send it by text or email, or visit the store.

5. Payment or cash on delivery

Choose your preferred payment option (cash on delivery, pay on pickup, bank transfer, or GCash) to be confirmed by the shop.

6. Delivery

Receive your items by pickup or delivery. Free delivery and cash on delivery, terms confirmed by the shop.

7. Feedback

Share your experience through the feedback form so the shop can continue improving service.
